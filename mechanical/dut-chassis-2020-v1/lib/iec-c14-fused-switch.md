The model uses the owner-approved 2026-09-07 side-profile evidence in private files `20260907_164602.jpg`, `20260907_164623.jpg`, `20260907_164636.jpg`, and `20260907_164702.jpg`. The first printed coupon was checked in private physical-fit photos `1-Photo-1.jpg`, `2-Photo-2.jpg`, and `3-Photo-3.jpg`. Only filenames identify provenance; no private image or metadata is committed.

The physical-fit correction establishes a 27 × 46.86 mm rear body, centred behind the 31 × 50.3 × 2 mm faceplate. Its top outline implements the owner-accepted literal 16 mm straight segment joined to two 8 mm diagonal edges. Plastic depth behind the front face is 21.8 mm. Both locking tongues occupy the same photographed broad wall. They are 5 mm high along the insertion-depth axis, span 8 and 5.5 mm along the 46.86 mm side-profile axis, and have top edges 10.5 mm behind the face datum. Their respective outer edges are inset 4.5 mm from the left and right body edges. Each tongue preserves 1 mm relief on its top, left, and right while its full bottom edge remains connected. The 4.5 mm value is lateral placement, never tab height. The outward tongue projection beyond the rigid body was not owner-measured; `iecc14_unmeasured_resting_tab_projection_assumption()` explicitly names the provisional 0.6 mm modeling assumption.

`iecc14_rigid_insertion_body_keepout()` is the compressed 27 × 46.86 mm insertion contract used by the panel-cutout negative. `iecc14_complete_rest_state_keepout()` adds both relaxed tongues and is the default exported `iecc14_keepout()` contract for enclosure clearance. Terminal lugs are schematic evidence only because their maximum projection is unmeasured and is excluded from both contracts.
