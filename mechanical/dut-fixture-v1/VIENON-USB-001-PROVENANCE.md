# VIENON Usb-001 model provenance

`vienon-usb-001.scad` is an original PocketForge reconstruction of the black
bottom-facing USB hub installed on the fixture. No listing image or third-party
geometry is redistributed.

## Identity and dimensions

- Owner-supplied listing: <https://www.amazon.com/dp/B09MLRPTT2>
- Brand/model: VIENON Usb-001, UPC 743890190214
- Product: ultra-slim four-port USB-A hub with fixed one-foot upstream lead
- Published envelope: 3.94 × 1.18 × 0.39 in, approximately
  100 × 30 × 10 mm
- Port population: one USB 3.0 plus three USB 2.0 data ports and blue status
  indicator. Gallery revisions color all four receptacle tongues blue; the
  model keeps one blue USB 3.0 tongue and three dark USB 2.0 tongues according
  to the listing's explicit interface specification.
- Fixture pose: four downstream ports face −Y/bottom and the fixed upstream
  lead exits +X/right, matching the owner photograph

The prior 105 × 24 mm analytical envelope was an allowed estimate. The exact
body is centred on the physically proven X tie axes. Only the presentation
body moves 2 mm toward the open plate edge; the printable tie slots are
unchanged.

## Geometry and license decision

Exact ASIN, UPC, model-number, STEP, STL, and CAD searches found no matching
model with a redistribution license. The source reconstructs the crowned ABS
shell, four receptacles, differentiated port tongues, LED, seam, fixed cable,
strain relief, and visible upstream USB-A plug.

Searches used `B09MLRPTT2`, `743890190214`, `VIENON Usb-001`, and their
combinations with `STEP`, `STL`, `CAD`, and `3D model` across general search
and public CAD-model indexes. No exact geometry candidate was found, so there
is no third-party author or license to carry forward; visually similar
unbranded four-port hubs were rejected as unverifiable revisions.
