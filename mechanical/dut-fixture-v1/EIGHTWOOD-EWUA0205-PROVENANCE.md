# Eightwood EWUA0205 model provenance

`eightwood-ewua0205.scad` is an original PocketForge reconstruction of the
single internal Wi-Fi antenna installed on the fixture. No listing image or
third-party geometry is redistributed.

## Identity and dimensions

- Owner-supplied listing: <https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0CRDVS774>
- Corroborating ended gallery:
  <https://www.ebay.com/itm/366200317542>
- Brand/model: Eightwood EWUA0205
- Product: two 5 dBi, 2.4/5 GHz internal antenna paddles for M.2/NGFF cards
- Installed count: one; the retail package contains two
- Listing dimensions: 114 × 15 mm paddle, 300 mm cable, Ø0.8 mm coax, and
  2.33 mm MHF4/IPEX connector
- Fixture pose: paddle horizontal, visible coax tail leaving the +X end

The prior fixture envelope was 110 × 14.3 mm and explicitly provisional.
The exact listing dimensional view supersedes that envelope. The two existing
tie axes remain at the same absolute X coordinates.

## Geometry and license decision

Exact ASIN, model-number, STEP, STL, and CAD searches found no matching model
with a license permitting repository redistribution. The source therefore
reconstructs the heat-shrink outline, necks, markings, visible coax service
tail, and MHF4 end from the preserved views. The full 300 mm lead is documented
but not coiled across the fixture presentation.

Searches used `B0CRDVS774`, `EWUA0205`, and their combinations with `STEP`,
`STL`, `CAD`, and `3D model` across general search and public CAD-model
indexes. No exact geometry candidate was found, so there is no third-party
author or license to carry forward; generic antenna paddles were rejected as
revision-ambiguous.
